Professional Borewell Drilling Services

High-precision DTH & rotary drilling engineered for maximum aquifer yield and well longevity.

Engineered Groundwater Discovery & Drilling

At BorewellPro, drilling a borewell is an exact scientific process. We combine geological resistivity aquifer mapping, high-pressure Down-The-Hole (DTH) percussion drilling, and heavy-duty casing systems to ensure your well delivers sustainable, crystal-clear water for decades to come.

Technical Specifications

Drill Hole Diameters 4.5", 6.0", 6.5", 7.5", and 8.5" diameter options
Maximum Depth Capability Up to 1,500 Feet (450+ Meters) into crystalline basement rocks
Drilling Technique DTH (Down-the-Hole) Hammer Percussion & Rotary Mud Drilling
Casing Options Heavy ISI-certified PVC Casing (Class-A) or Mild Steel (MS) Welded Pipes
Operating Air Pressure High-pressure screw air compressors up to 350 PSI / 1200 CFM
Strata Compatibility Hard granite, gneiss, limestone, sandstone, clay, and alluvial gravel

Our Drilling Workflow

01

Site Hydrogeology Survey

Resistivity tests determine subterranean fissure frequency and aquifer depth.

02

Casing Lowering & Soil Sealing

Top soil layer is cased and sealed to prevent loose soil collapse and surface water runoff.

03

Deep Rock Drilling

Tungsten-carbide button bits drill through hard rock until water veins are unlocked.

04

Air Flushing & Yield Testing

All rock cuttings are cleared, and water discharge is measured with V-notch weir.
