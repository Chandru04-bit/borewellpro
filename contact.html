What Happens During a Site Visit?

  • Terrain & Access Verification: We verify driveway clearance, turning radius, and overhead cables for truck or crawler rigs.
  • Geophysical Resistivity Survey: Calibrated electrodes scan subsurface fractures to calculate groundwater depth and yield.
  • Written Feasibility Estimate: You receive an itemized estimate with casing depth, per-foot drill costs, and pump recommendations.
