Pollachi Coconut Estate Agricultural Borewell

Deep 850 Ft fractured bedrock agricultural well equipped with a 7.5 HP solar pump delivering continuous irrigation to 25 acres.

Request Farm Survey
Solar Array Synced & Heavy MS Casing Installed Depth: 850 Ft | 7.5 HP Solar

High-Yield Solar-Powered Farm Irrigation

In the fertile coconut farming belt of Pollachi, sustainable water security is essential. Our team performed comprehensive electrical resistivity depth profiling, discovering deep fractured aquifer zones at 820–850 feet. Equipped with heavy welded Mild Steel (MS) casing and a 7.5 HP solar-powered brushless submersible pump, this agricultural well delivers round-the-clock water to 25 acres of coconut groves and intercrops.

Key Project Highlights

Deep fractured aquifer discovery at 850 feet in hard gneissic rock
Seamless integration with high-efficiency 7.5 HP solar arrays
Zero-grid-reliance agricultural irrigation for 25+ acres
Welded heavy duty MS casing (110 Ft) prevents surface boulder shifts
Dual-point 350 PSI high-velocity air compressor desilting & flushing
Sustained discharge rate of 8,500+ Litres Per Hour confirmed by flow testing

Project Engineering Specifications

Project Location Pollachi Coconut Estate & Agro Farm, Tamil Nadu
Total Drilled Depth 850 Feet (259.1 Meters)
Discharge Yield Rate 3.5 Inch Heavy Flow (~8,500 Litres Per Hour)
Drilling Technology 6.5" Super-Drill DTH Air Percussion with 1200 CFM Compressor
Casing Pipe Installed Heavy Duty Welded Mild Steel (MS) Casing (110 Ft)
Pumping Equipment 7.5 HP Solar DC Submersible Motor with MPPT Solar Inverter
Geological Formation Gneissic Bedrock with Quartz Vein Inclusions
Irrigation Coverage 25+ Acres Drip & Basin Micro-Irrigation
