Commercial Complex Erode, Tamil Nadu

Erode Commercial Center High-Capacity Borewell

Engineered 620 Ft high-capacity automated borewell providing continuous water supply for a multi-tenant commercial mall and office complex.

Automated VFD Panel Commissioned & 100% Tenant Occupancy Ready Depth: 620 Ft | Smart VFD

Automated Multi-Storey Commercial Water Setup

For this bustling multi-storey shopping and office complex in Erode, our hydrogeologists mapped multiple high-permeability fissures at 590 to 620 feet. The site was engineered with computerized digital pressure transducers, automated dry-run protectors, and heavy ISI casing, ensuring zero downtime for hundreds of daily commercial tenants, employees, and retail visitors.

Key Project Highlights

Engineered specifically for multi-tenant high-density commercial occupancy
Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) pressure modulation for power efficiency
Integrated automated dry-run sensors and digital tank overflow cutoffs
Heavy Class-A PVC casing (90 Ft) with sanitary annular cement grout
Dual overhead gravity-feed delivery riser lines to roof reservoirs
Comprehensive 16-parameter drinking and sanitary water safety certification

Project Engineering Specifications

Project Location Erode Commercial Mall & Office Complex, Tamil Nadu
Total Drilled Depth 620 Feet (188.9 Meters)
Discharge Yield Rate 3.0 Inch Stable Output (~6,000 Litres Per Hour)
Drilling Technology 6.5" High-Velocity DTH Percussion with Compact Crawler Rig
Casing Pipe Installed Heavy Class-A PVC Casing with Sanitary Annular Grout (90 Ft)
Pumping Equipment 5.0 HP High-Head Submersible Motor with Smart VFD Panel
Geological Formation Weathered Gneiss transitioning to Solid Hornblende Granite
Automation System Smart Pressure Transducer, Auto-Level Cutoff & Dry-Run Shield
